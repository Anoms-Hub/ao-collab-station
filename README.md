AO Collab Station
The official collaboration portal for the AO Universe.

Everything Connected. Everything Designed. Everything AO.

The AO Collab Station is the central entry point for creators, builders, storytellers, guardians, hosts, and contributors who want to participate in the AO Universe. This portal serves as the unified onboarding and activation hub for all collaboration across AO’s 12 divisions.

This repo contains the frontend code for the AO Collaboration Station website, deployed to anomartzy.xyz and maintained inside the Anoms‑Hub GitHub Enterprise Organization.
🌌 Purpose

The AO Collab Station exists to:

    Provide a single, unified portal for community involvement

    Allow people to choose their role in the AO Universe

    Collect interest and route contributors to the correct AO division

    Serve as the public-facing gateway into AO’s creative‑tech ecosystem

    Support AO’s mission of building a connected, neon‑coded universe

🧩 Roles in the AO Universe

Visitors can choose from six primary collaboration paths:

    AO Creators — designers, illustrators, visual artists

    AO Builders — coders, automation engineers, AI toolmakers

    AO Storytellers — writers, editors, educators, content creators

    AO Guardians — cybersecurity, IP protection, moderation

    AO Community Hosts — event leaders, facilitators, community builders

    AO Contributors — general support, volunteers, explorers

Each role connects to one or more AO divisions.
